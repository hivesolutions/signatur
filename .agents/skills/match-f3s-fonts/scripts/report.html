How to read it

  • Spacing: glyph centre error after removing the mean offset of each line, the measure of the TTF spacing against the F3S one.
  • Line width: error of the distance between the first and the last glyph of each line.
  • Baseline: vertical error of each line, the measure of the size and the line height.
  • F3S match: share of the F3S strokes, drawn at the payload size, found on the engraving; low values mean another font or size was engraved (Gravostyle resizes text that overflows the area).
  • Absolute: raw centre error, which also holds the line centring (Gravostyle centres each line on its ink, the viewport on its advances), reported only.
  • Overlay: Gravostyle ink, viewport ink, both, at the same millimetre scale on the whole plate.
